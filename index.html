How the numbers are calculated

  • Manual weekly minutes = Σ (units/week × manual minutes/unit)
  • Current weekly human minutes = Σ (units/week × human minutes/unit with bot) + maintenance + additional LinkedIn
  • Net weekly minutes saved = manual weekly minutes − current weekly human minutes
  • Reduction % = net weekly minutes saved ÷ manual weekly minutes × 100
  • Setup payback weeks = setup hours ÷ (net weekly minutes saved ÷ 60)

Values are computed at full precision and rounded only for display. Career-page monitoring keeps its exact 0.125 minutes per check (40 checks = 20 companies checked twice weekly = 5 minutes total per week).

What this does and does not show

  • The comparison estimates the manual effort for the same weekly workload and assumes comparable output quality.
  • It does not establish that I previously spent exactly that much time, nor that output quality has been verified.
  • Only active human time is counted — review, correction, and maintenance — not unattended bot runtime, and without double counting.
  • Results are estimates based on the assumptions entered here, not verified productivity gains. No claim is made about interviews, offers, or hiring outcomes.
  • Inputs are saved in this browser via local storage. They do not sync to other devices and do not change what other visitors see.
